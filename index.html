Expenses

An Android app that imports your bank statement, lets AI sort every purchase, and splits shared costs in a tap.

  1. Tap Import Excel to load a sample bank statement.
  2. Mark each row: I Pay Full, Half, She Pays or Skip. Tap any text on a card to edit it.
  3. Tap Finish to see the Telegram message it would send.
  4. Look around History, Charts and Budget.

Everything here is sample data and stays in your browser. Reload the page to start over.
